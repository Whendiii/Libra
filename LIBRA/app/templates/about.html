About LIBRA

Library Integrated Borrowing, Research, and Analytics is a research discovery and library support system designed for the Isabela State University – Echague Campus research community.

ABOUT LIBRA

Making previous research easier to discover.

LIBRA provides students and researchers with a convenient way to explore thesis and capstone projects available in the university library.

Instead of manually going through research records, users can search the collection using information such as thesis titles, authors, programs, research fields, topics, and keywords.

LIBRA STANDS FOR

Library Integrated Borrowing, Research, and Analytics

A centralized system for discovering research and supporting library borrowing activities.

OUR PURPOSE

Connecting researchers with existing knowledge.

LIBRA aims to make the university's research collection more accessible and organized while providing a clearer process for locating and borrowing available research materials.

By making previous studies easier to find, the system can help students explore existing topics, identify related research, and discover studies that may support the development of their own academic work.

WHAT LIBRA OFFERS

Research discovery in one place.

Core features designed to support research exploration and library services.

Search & Discovery

Find previous research using thesis titles, authors, programs, research fields, topics, and keywords.

Thesis Information

View important research information including authors, abstract, keywords, publication year, and library availability.

Borrowing Support

Submit borrowing information through the system and use the generated borrowing slip for the library borrowing process.

Related Research

Discover other studies connected to the research being viewed through related topics, fields, and research information.

Library Analytics

Help library administrators understand search activity, borrowing trends, popular research topics, and collection usage.

Collection Availability

Check whether a research material is available or currently borrowed and identify its shelf location before visiting the library.

FOR THE ISU–ECHAGUE RESEARCH COMMUNITY

Explore. Discover. Build on research.

LIBRA supports students and researchers by making previous academic work easier to discover while helping the campus library maintain a more organized research and borrowing environment.
